# antislop audit 001 (2026-11-20)

Scope: full site (`src/`, `index.html`), read against `DESIGN.md` and `PRODUCT.md`.
Method: code inspection plus computed WCAG contrast ratios. The app was not run for this audit; R-35 click-through happens after the fixes.

Design Read: editorial portfolio for recruiters and hiring managers, "Printed Issue" language, dials ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 3 (taken from DESIGN.md: numbered spreads, alternating feature rows, GSAP pin and scrub).

Priority: HIGH = Hard Gate, MEDIUM = Purpose-Gate, LOW = Quality Lock / craftsmanship.

---

## HIGH

**1. Case-study hero text fails contrast on two client colours** (R-25)
`WorkDetails.vue:5` puts `text-light` (#e8ebf0) on the per-project colour from `PortfolioData.js`.
- Kappartner App `bg-[#00aeb3]` (`PortfolioData.js:365`): 2.28:1 at full strength and 1.92:1 for the 80% "Back to Works" link. This fails even the large-text threshold.
- Lydia's Lechon and Lydia's Lechon Style Guide `bg-[#018441]` (`PortfolioData.js:284`, `:306`): 4.01:1, which fails for the `text-sm` meta values and the 0.625rem keys.
Fix options: add a per-project `textColor` field (ink on #00aeb3 gives about 8:1), or darken only the hero plate.

**2. Home meta keys sit below the opacity floor** (R-25)
`Home.vue:66`, `:70`: the `<dt>` labels "Practice" and "Scope" use `opacity-60`, which measures 4.39:1 on Newsprint Grey. DESIGN.md's own Opacity Floor Rule sets the floor at 0.65.

**3. Hidden pager arrows still take keyboard focus** (R-32)
`WebView.vue:13-45` + `main.css:221`: `.is-hidden` sets only `opacity-0 pointer-events-none`. At either end of the pager, Tab lands on an invisible button that does nothing. Add `:disabled` (or `tabindex="-1"` + `aria-hidden`) when hidden.

**4. Em dashes in prose and metadata** (R-02)
DESIGN.md does not prescribe these:
- `About.vue:59`: "I'm Jano — a UI/UX designer…"
- `Contact.vue:25`: "…or a half-baked concept — drop a note…"
- `PortfolioData.js:545`: "what makes her stand out—not just as an idol…"
- `index.html:11, 12, 17, 24, 29, 32, 125`: page `<title>`, meta description, OG/Twitter titles and alts, JSON-LD name. These appear in browser tabs and LinkedIn share cards.

**5. DESIGN.md prescribes an em dash in chapter labels. Owner decision needed.** (R-02 vs R-37 conflict)
DESIGN.md asks for `04 — BIOGRAPHY` and `— PHOTOGRAPHED IN BULACAN`. In code: `Home.vue:53`, `Features.vue:11`, `:30`, `Works.vue:8`, `:110`, `About.vue:33`, `:46`, `Contact.vue:11`, `:17`, `:76`, `NotFound.vue:6`.
Options: **keep** (I record the override and leave them), or **swap** to a separator that keeps the print feel (`04 / Biography`, `04 · Biography`, `No. 04 Biography`).

**6. "Avg. response · 24 hours" has no source** (R-17, R-36)
`Contact.vue` form footer. PRODUCT.md's evidence list does not include a response-time figure. If the figure is true, keep it and add it to PRODUCT.md. If not, remove it or reword it as a commitment ("I reply within a day").

**7. Stat counts don't trace to a list** (R-17, R-38)
`About.vue:196`: `technologies: 14, tools: 10`.
- PRODUCT.md says 13+ technologies.
- The About marquee lists 12 technologies and 7 tools.
- "Est. 2021" (Home) and "3+ years" (About) also disagree. 2021 to 2026 is 5 years, and CoreProc since 2022 is 4.
Pick one source list, then derive the numbers from it in code so they can't drift.

## MEDIUM

**8. Buzzwords and stock phrasing** (R-16)
PRODUCT.md sets the voice as "confident, technical, low word count".
- `About.vue:53-55`: "Designing **Seamless** Journeys, One Pixel At A Time." This combines a banned word with a cliché, in the panel's main heading.
- `PortfolioData.js` feature copy:
  - "passionate", "stunning vector art", "fresh, innovative concepts"
  - "bringing ideas to life" (twice)
  - "captivate and resonate", "leave a lasting impression", "high-impact videos"
- `Contact.vue:25`: "idea worth chasing… turn it into something real" is pitched at freelance clients. PRODUCT.md says the primary reader is a recruiter and freelance acquisition is undecided.

**9. "View Project" uses an external-link icon** (R-04)
`Works.vue` card overlay uses `fa-arrow-up-right-from-square`, but the card opens an in-page sheet or modal, so the glyph promises the wrong behaviour.

**10. Dot-grid backdrop has no written purpose** (R-07)
`main.css:290`, used in `Home.vue` and `Contact.vue`. DESIGN.md mentions it under Elevation but never says what it serves. Graph paper also pulls against the "Printed Issue" metaphor. Write one line of purpose into DESIGN.md, or drop it.

**11. About drops Vector from the discipline list** (C-1, PRODUCT Principle 2)
`About.vue` meta grid shows "Disciplines: Web · UI/UX · Motion", while the stat beside it says 4 and "range is the argument". Video and vector need to show as two separate disciplines.

## LOW

**12. Contact and CV are not "always near"** (C-3, PRODUCT Principle 1)
Download CV exists only on About (panel 04). Home has no route to Contact or CV, so a recruiter who stops at the first screen has neither exit.

**13. CV download is a JS-built link inside a `<button>`** (C-2)
`About.vue:149`, `:247`: you can't middle-click it or copy the link, and it fails without JS. Use `<a href="/downloads/…pdf" download class="btn btn-primary">`.

**14. Works card re-declares its shadows inline** (Quality Lock, DESIGN Elevation)
`Works.vue:51` hand-types the rest and lifted shadow values plus raw `rgba()` borders, instead of using `.surface-rest` / `.surface-lifted`. DESIGN.md says "don't write raw hex outside @theme".

**15. SkeletonLoader rounding and grey** (R-11)
It has 40 uses of `rounded-2xl` and one `bg-gray-300`. DESIGN.md already lists this as a known holdout. Fix it while the area is open.

**16. Tool-logo names appear only in a `title` tooltip** (C-4)
`Features.vue` `.tool-pill`: the name shows only on mouse hover. Screen readers get the `alt`, but keyboard and touch users never see it. Consider a visible caption or a focusable chip.

## Checked and clean

- No testimonials, FAQ, pricing, fake logos, or invented clients (R-18, R-28, R-36).
- Every nav item maps to a real panel. Social links are live, and `mailto:` is real (R-24, R-26).
- The global focus ring is 2px accent with an ink inversion on accent plates. The removed outlines on Works cards and inputs have replacements (R-32).
- The form has loading, success, error, and per-field validation states. Works has an empty filter state. Case studies have a skeleton, and images resolve on error (R-27).
- Light and dark themes are both specified, contrast-checked in DESIGN.md, and FOUC-guarded (R-21, R-34).
- Reduced motion is honoured, and motion has a written purpose throughout (R-19).
- The palette is 2 neutrals + 1 accent per theme, plus 4 informational category colours (R-29).
- Strong identity: sharp plates, round controls, numbered chapters, outlined display type (R-20).
